## Approach #6 Results: Segment Deduplication

### What was done
Added a post-processing `deduplicate_segments()` step after all LLM corrections. Uses `difflib.SequenceMatcher` to detect duplicates within a sliding window of 3 consecutive segments. Two removal criteria:
1. **Exact duplicates**: segments with identical text — remove the later one
2. **High similarity** (ratio ≥ 0.9): near-duplicate segments — remove the shorter one

### Key numbers
- **White box**: 78.2% → 80.7% (+2.5%). Removes seg 40 `快找我聊天` (exact dup of seg 38) and seg 47 `我怎麼粉碎你頭開頭` (0.95 similar to seg 46 `看我怎麼粉碎你頭開頭`)
- **Black box**: ~70.9% → ~71.4% (+0.5%)
- White box original transcription similarity also improved: 75.9% (was ~72% before dedup of raw transcription is also visible in comparison)

### Findings and quality notes
- **Conservative thresholds are essential**: Substring removal and lower similarity thresholds (0.7-0.8) caused 2-3% drops in black box score by removing legitimate speech repetitions
- **Short fragment removal is risky**: Removing segments ≤ 2-3 chars that are substrings of nearby segments helped white box but hurt black box — short segments can be legitimate
- **Seg 44 `看我` not removed**: This 2-char fragment could be deduped but the safe threshold doesn't catch it. Removing it helps white box (+0.8%) but hurts black box
- **LLM noise**: Results vary ±0.3% between runs due to LLM non-determinism. The dedup improvement is clearly above noise level for white box but borderline for black box

## Approach #7 Results: Number Format Normalization

### What was done
Added a post-processing `normalize_numbers()` step after deduplication. Converts Chinese numerals (二–十, compound like 二十三) to Arabic digits when followed by counter/unit words (年, 小時, 個, 班, etc.). Key design decision: **excludes 一** from conversion, since in subtitle context it almost always means "a/an" rather than the number "1" (一個月, 一步之遙, 下一首 all stay as-is in CC).

### Key numbers
- **White box**: 80.7% → 81.2% (+0.5%). Three conversions: `六小時→6小時` (seg 2), `三年→3年` (seg 9), `二年→2年` (seg 42)
- **Black box**: 71.4–71.9% (baseline ~71.4%, at worst neutral, sometimes +0.5%)
- White box is deterministic and consistent across runs; black box variation is LLM noise

### Findings and quality notes
- **Excluding 一 is critical**: First iteration included 一 and converted `一個月→1個月`, `一步→1步`, `下一首→下1首` — all wrong per CC. Dropped white box from 80.7% to 80.5%
- **Compound counters needed**: `小時`, `分鐘`, `公里` etc. need special handling since the counter is multi-character
- **Safe approach**: Only converts numerals immediately followed by counters. No false positives observed in either test set
